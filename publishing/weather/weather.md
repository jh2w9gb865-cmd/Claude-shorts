## Song

**Title:** What's the Weather?

**Lyrics**

```
Sunny, sunny, shining bright,
warm and yellow, golden light!
Put your sunhat on your head,
let's go play outside instead!

What's the weather? Let's go see!
Look outside with you and me!
Sunny, rainy, windy, snow,
every day a different show!

Rainy, rainy, pitter-pat,
grab your boots and grab your hat!
Jump in puddles, splish, splash, splash,
here comes thunder, flash, flash, flash!

Windy, windy, whoosh, whoosh, whoosh,
leaves go flying, swoosh, swoosh, swoosh!
Hold your kite and hold on tight,
up it goes, a windy flight!

Snowy, snowy, cold and white,
build a snowman, what a sight!
Mittens on and scarf so warm,
catch a snowflake, watch it form!

Hooray for weather!
```

**Characters**
- ☀️ **Sunny**: warm, bright and always smiling
- ☁️ **Puffy Cloud**: brings the rain and the wind
- 💧 **Drippy**: a raindrop who loves puddles
- ❄️ **Flurry**: a sparkly little snowflake
