## Song

**Title:** Brush Brush Brush Your Teeth

**Lyrics**

```
Wake up, teeth, it's time to shine,
let's brush them clean, they'll look so fine!
A little toothpaste, pea-size dot,
brush the front and back a lot!

Brush, brush, brush your teeth,
top and bottom, round and round!
Brush, brush, brush your teeth,
sparkly smiles all around!

Brush the top and brush the bottom,
tiny bubbles, now you've got 'em!
Little circles, round and round,
brushy, brushy, hear that sound?

Don't forget your tongue so pink,
swish and spit into the sink!
Two whole minutes, then we're done,
brushing teeth is so much fun!

Sparkly smile!
```

**Characters**
- 🪥 **Bristle**: a speedy little toothbrush
- 🦷 **Toothy**: a happy tooth who loves to sparkle
- 🧴 **Minty**: a minty fresh toothpaste tube
- 🐰 **Bella Bunny**: has the brightest smile in town
