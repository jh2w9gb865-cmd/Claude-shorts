## Song

**Title:** Farm Animal Sounds

**Lyrics**

```
The cow in the barn says, 'Moo, moo, moo!'
She gives us milk, it's good for you!
The pig in the mud says, 'Oink, oink, oink!'
He rolls around and gives a wink!

On the farm, on the farm,
hear the animals by the barn!
Moo, oink, quack and baa,
neigh, neigh, ha, ha, ha!

The duck in the pond says, 'Quack, quack, quack!'
She waddles there and waddles back!
The sheep on the hill says, 'Baa, baa, baa!'
Her woolly coat is soft, ah-ah!

The horse in the field says, 'Neigh, neigh, neigh!'
He gallops fast and loves to play!
Now all together, loud and proud,
let's make our farm sounds really loud!

Ha, ha, hooray!
```

**Characters**
- 🐄 **Daisy Cow**: says moo and gives us milk
- 🐷 **Pinky Pig**: loves rolling in the mud
- 🦆 **Ducky**: waddles to the pond
- 🐑 **Woolly Sheep**: has the fluffiest coat
- 🐴 **Henry Horse**: gallops fast across the field
