## Song

**Title:** Wash Wash Wash Your Hands

**Lyrics**

```
Turn the water on, drip, drop,
squeeze the soap, pump, pump, pop!
Rub your hands and make it foam,
bubbles, bubbles, all your own!

Wash, wash, wash your hands,
scrub them clean today!
Wash, wash, wash your hands,
wash the germs away!

Palms together, rub, rub, rub,
backs and fingers, scrub, scrub, scrub!
Don't forget your thumbs and nails,
wiggle, wiggle, bubble trails!

Little germs say, 'Oh, oh, no!'
Time for all the germs to go!
Rinse them off and shake, shake, shake,
dry them with a towel, no mistake!

Clean hands!
```

**Characters**
- 🧼 **Sudsy Soap**: makes the biggest, foamiest bubbles
- 🫧 **Bubbles**: a shiny bubble who floats around
- 🦠 **Grubby the Germ**: a silly little germ who doesn't like soap
- 🐰 **Bella Bunny**: always washes her paws
