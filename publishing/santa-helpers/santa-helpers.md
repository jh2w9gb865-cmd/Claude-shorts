## Song

**Title:** Santa's Little Helpers

**Lyrics**

```
In the workshop, tap, tap, tap,
elves are busy, wrap, wrap, wrap!
Paper, ribbon, bows on top,
Santa's helpers never stop!

Ho, ho, ho, here we go,
Santa's helpers in the snow!
Wrap a present, make a toy,
share the love and share the joy!

Reindeer stretch and stand in line,
Gingerbread says, 'Looking fine!'
Load the sleigh up, one, two, three,
gifts for you and gifts for me!

Santa smiles and waves goodbye,
watch the sleigh fly through the sky!
Be kind and share, and you will see,
the best gift is you and me!

Ho, ho, ho!
```

**Characters**
- 🧝 **Elfie**: wraps presents faster than anyone
- 🎅 **Santa**: jolly, kind and always smiling
- 🦌 **Rudy Reindeer**: ready to pull the sleigh
- 🧝 **Jingles**: ties the prettiest bows
- 🍪 **Ginger**: the sweetest helper in the workshop
