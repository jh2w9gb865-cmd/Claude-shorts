## Song

**Title:** Count to 10 with Froggy

**Lyrics**

```
One, two, Froggy says, 'Hello to you!'
Three, four, hop across the floor!
Five, six, jumping tricks!
Seven, eight, Froggy's feeling great!

Count with me from one to ten,
jump and count it once again!
One, two, three, four, five,
six, seven, eight, nine, TEN!

One little ladybug, red and small,
two little chicks that peep and call,
three bluebirds singing in a tree,
four little foxes, count with me!

Five, six, seven, jump in line,
eight and nine, we're doing fine!
And the last one, here we go,
TEN! The biggest jump, you know!

We counted to ten!
```

**Characters**
- 🐞 **Lily Ladybug**: small, red and full of spots
- 🐥 **Pip the Chick**: peeps every number
- 🐸 **Freddie Frog**: jumps once for every number
- 🐦 **Bobby Bluebird**: counts from up in the tree
- 🦊 **Finn Fox**: counts with his fluffy tail
