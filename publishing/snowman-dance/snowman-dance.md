## Song

**Title:** Snowman Dance

**Lyrics**

```
I'm a snowman, round and white,
with a carrot nose so bright.
When the music starts to play,
I can dance the snowy way!

Shake, shake, shake your arms,
stomp, stomp, stomp your feet,
wiggle, wiggle, turn around,
dance the snowman beat!

Penguin flaps and Bunny hops,
spinning round like spinning tops.
Clap your hands and touch your nose,
wiggle all your frosty toes!

Nod your head and touch the ground,
jump up high and spin around!
Snowflakes falling, one, two, three,
come and dance the snow with me!

Yay, snow!
```
