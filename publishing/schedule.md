# Sunny Sprouts — upload schedule (YouTube, US audience)

Times are in Yerevan time (UTC+4). US Eastern Time is 9 hours behind Yerevan from November to
early March, and 8 hours behind from mid-March (US daylight saving), so Yerevan times shift 1 hour earlier then.

## Weekly rhythm: 2 new songs a week (A and B), 4 uploads

| Day | Yerevan time | US Eastern | Upload |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 17:00 | 8:00 AM | Short: song A |
| Wednesday | 17:00 | 8:00 AM | Short: song B |
| Friday | 18:00 | 9:00 AM | Regular 16:9 video: song A |
| Sunday | 18:00 | 9:00 AM | Regular 16:9 video: song B |

- Once there are 5–6 songs: post a **compilation** (10–15 min, all songs back-to-back) on a Saturday at 18:00, then once a month after that.
- Claude sends a reminder with all files about 15–20 minutes before each upload.
