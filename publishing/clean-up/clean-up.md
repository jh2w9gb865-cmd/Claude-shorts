## Song

**Title:** Clean Up Song

**Lyrics**

```
Toys are here and toys are there,
toys are scattered everywhere!
Let's all help and play a game,
every toy goes home again!

Clean up, clean up, everybody help,
put the toys back on the shelf!
Clean up, clean up, one, two, three,
a tidy room for you and me!

Blocks go in the big red bin,
Teddy waves and jumps right in!
Balls go bouncing to the shelf,
look, I did it by myself!

Books stand up all in a row,
crayons back in the box they go!
Look around, it's nice and neat,
clean-up time is such a treat!

All clean!
```

**Characters**
- 🧸 **Teddy Bear**: always first to help
- ⚽ **Bouncy Ball**: bounces back to the shelf
- 🧱 **Blocky**: a toy block who loves a tidy box
- 🐰 **Bella Bunny**: puts the books back in a row
